About

I am a Doctoral Researcher under the supervision of Dr. Husney Parvez Sarwar as a Senior Research Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IIT Kharagpur). I completed my 5-year integrated M.Sc. from Aliah University (Newtown Campus, Kolkata).

My research interest lies in commutative algebra and algebraic K-theory, with a focus on subintegrality and seminormality of ring extensions and their K-theoretic invariants — particularly the Picard group and the group of invertible submodules. My thesis extends this framework to extensions of monoid algebras, graded integral domains, and Rees-like algebras, alongside related results on projective modules and Serre dimension.
